# Project and participants

A **project** is one application built for one customer. It gathers the [specification](specification.md), the [stories](stories.md) and the [versions](release.md) that come from it.

A project belongs to its customer, from the first day and whatever happens to the maker afterwards.

## Participants

- The **customer** — commissions the application and owns it. They decide what is wanted.
- The **maker** — builds and maintains it, and writes the specification down.
- The **domain expert** — knows how the business actually works, and whose words the specification uses. When a term is disputed, their usage wins.

One person may hold several of these roles, and usually does on a small project. The roles say what someone knows, not what they are allowed to touch.

## Rules

**The specification is the source of truth.** The application is a consequence of it, never the other way round.

**Nothing is agreed by silence.** Agreement is an act, by the customer, on something named, on a date.

**No part of a project may depend on Supersoft continuing to exist.** A project abandoned by its maker, and by Supersoft, remains an application another maker can pick up by reading its specification.
